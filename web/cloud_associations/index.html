How do action words pair with topic words?

This site reads 347 syllabi sentence by sentence and groups their verbs and keywords into categories. The heatmap shows which kinds of doing are associated with which kinds of topics. Hover over a cell to see the courses that use that pairing most; click a cell to see how the pairing has changed over time; click any course to see its full profile.

More information

Implementation Details

Every syllabus was split into sentences and the quantity of words in each verb and keyword category were counted. A sentence counts toward a verb group when it uses one of that group's verbs, and toward a keyword group when it mentions one of that group's topic words. Words are lemmatized to match their dictionary base form, and a word only counts as a keyword when it isn't being used as a verb in that sentence — so "building a wall" counts as a verb, while "the building" counts as a keyword. "Distinctiveness of Association" compares how often two word groups actually share a sentence with how often they would if the pairing were independent — a methodology known as Pointwise Mutual Information. The baseline is computed only over sentences that contain at least one categorized word, so administrative boilerplate (dates, grading policies, schedules) doesn't affect it.

THE FOUR FIELDS

Design Studio

Courses centered on the development of an architectural, urban, or landscape design proposal. The final work gives form to the project through representation, revealing its relationships and interactions with its surroundings. Projects may vary in scale and focus.

Architectural Practice

Courses centered on the designer’s role as a professional. Professional practice, the regulation and management of the built environment, and the designer’s ethical responsibilities form the core of the inquiry.

Technology & Representation

Courses with a particular focus on materials, construction techniques, structures, and environmental performance, closely intertwined with tools and technologies of representation. These become the media through which the project is explored and communicated.

History & Theory

Courses centered on history, theory, criticism, and social and cultural studies. They examine how change over time has shaped the present, seeking to understand the complex systems and relationships that underpin it.

How the association is calculated

The ratio is observed shared sentences × baseline sentences ÷ (verb sentences × keyword sentences), calculated within the selected collection. As in the reference explorer, the baseline uses active sentences when available and otherwise all sentences for each syllabus. Cells with fewer than three shared sentences are not scored. Hidden topic categories match the reference: local, universal and theory.
